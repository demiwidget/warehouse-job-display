# Office Gift sound pack

The files `office-gift-01.wav` through `office-gift-06.wav` are short clips derived from
"Farting sound effects.webm" on Wikimedia Commons:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Farting_sound_effects.webm

The source is dedicated to the public domain under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0
Universal Public Domain Dedication. The clips were converted to WAV, trimmed, and
volume-normalized for use by the Warehouse Dashboard system.
